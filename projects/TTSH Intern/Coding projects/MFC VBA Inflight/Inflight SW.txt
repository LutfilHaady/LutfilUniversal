2)	You agree to pay for all charges related to my admission as reflected on the final bill.
3)	You acknowledge that you have been advised on the following points regarding patient estimated care cost:
a.	You have been advised on patient estimated case cost based on past bill sizes for the medical investigations, treatments and procedures in question
b.	Patient final bill will include all charges for medical investigations, doctors’ fees, warding, treatments and implants, where applicable. Depending on patient medical condition, the final cost may differ from the estimated care cost.
c.	If the hospital faced a sudden and overwhelming surge in the number of patients, temporary beds may be activated so that appropriate and timely care can be administered to patients. The same ward charges will apply.
These beds may be located in the main hospital block, National Centre for Infectious Diseases (NCID) or TTSH wards at Renci Community Hospital.
4)	Change of Ward Type
a.	To a higher ward type
i.	Subject to bed availability.
ii.	Higher rate effective from date of transfer for General Ward Fee.
iii.	Higher rate effective from date of admission for Service Charges.
b.	To a lower ward type
i.	Subject to means testing and bed availability
ii.	Lower rate effective from date of transfer for both General Ward Fee and Service Charges
c.	Use of Specialised Treatment Areas
i.	Higher ward charges may apply if patient require treatment in specialized treatment areas such as High Dependency Units (HDUs), Intensive Care Units (ICUs), or Isolation Wards.
d.	Overstaying
i.	To ensure that hospital beds are available to patient requiring acute care, Government subsidy, MediShield Life or Medifund will not be extended further from the 7th calendar day after the patient is certified to be medically fit for discharge from the hospital, if the patient decides to remain in the hospital. Integrated Shield Plans (IPs) ad Civil Service Medical Benefits will similarly not be extended further.

5)	MediShield Life (MSHL) / Integrated Shield Plan (IP)
a.	All Singapore Citizens (SCs) and Permanent Residents (PRs) are covered under MSHL. Patients with IPs may enjoy higher coverage provided by their private insurers, subject to the benefits under their IP policies
b.	MSHL / IP payouts for the patient’s hospitalization bills will be computed based on scheme parameters such as claim limits, deductibles and co-insurance.
c.	A list of standard exclusions (e.g. cosmetic treatment) apply under MediShield / Integrated Shield Plans. Treatments under this list will not be covered.
6)	All pre-existing medical conditions are covered under MSHL. However, the patient’s private insurer may impose exclusions. Please check with patient private insurer for more information if needed.
7)	MediSave (MSV)
a.	Patients may use their own MediSave or cash to pay for the remainder of their bill after subsidies (if applicable) and MediShield Life/Integrated Shield Plan coverage. Family members can also use their MediSave to pay for their approved dependents. More than one of these payment modes may be combined.
*MSV is capped at the withdrawal limit.
b.	MSV cannot be used for: 
i.	Hospitalization stay of less than 8 hours (except day surgeries)
ii.	Pre-admission Tests when admission is cancelled
iii.	Treatments for non-medical purposes (e.g. cosmetic surgeries)
iv.	Appliance (e.g. crutches/ walking frames)
v.	Charges incurred at the Accident & Emergency (A&E) department.
8)	Financial Assistance
a.	SC/PR subsidized patients who require financial assistance may approach out staff for more information.
9)	You understand the hospital is committed to providing further care cost advice and to providing me with an updated estimated care cost if the bill size is likely to change significantly due to the development of patient medical condition and/or increase in length of stay.
10)	Itemized bills can be provided at the end of patient admission, upon request.
11)	Patient personal data, including medical information, may be disclosed and used by the hospital and its appointed agents for bill collection, or to facilitate payments to the patient and/or and individual.
